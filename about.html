About OJUYS at Home

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Who We Are

OJUYS at Home is an independent resource for people planning home renovations. We publish practical guides, honest material comparisons, and free planning calculators — no fluff, no sponsorships that sway our judgment.

What We Cover

Renovation planning, budgeting tools, material selection (tiles, paint, wallpaper, flooring), kitchen and bathroom design, aging-in-place modifications, indoor air quality, sustainable and smart home upgrades, solar energy, water systems, and global design trends.

Our Approach

Every article and calculator is researched and written to help you make informed decisions. We prioritize accuracy, clarity, and usefulness over trends or affiliate revenue.
